CHURN PREDICTION MODEL
======================

Training Date: 2024
Model Type: XGBoost Classifier
Training Data: 93,357 customers

FEATURES USED:
- frequency (total orders)
- monetary (total spent)
- avg_order_value (mean spend per order)
- avg_items (avg items per order)
- avg_freight (avg freight value)
- installments (avg payment installments)
- avg_review (avg review score)
- pct_late (late delivery rate)
- avg_delivery (avg delivery days)
- lifespan_days (days between first and last order)

PERFORMANCE:
- ROC-AUC: 0.8483
- Precision (Churned): 0.89
- Recall (Churned): 0.74
- F1-Score (Churned): 0.81
- Accuracy: 0.75

CHURN DEFINITION:
Churn = No purchase in 180 days

CLASS BALANCE:
- Churn rate: 70.7% (65,962 churned / 27,395 active)
- Handled with: scale_pos_weight in XGBoost

HYPERPARAMETERS:
- n_estimators: 300
- max_depth: 5
- learning_rate: 0.05
- subsample: 0.8
- colsample_bytree: 0.8
- scale_pos_weight: 0.415 (ratio of negatives to positives)
